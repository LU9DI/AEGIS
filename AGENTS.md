# Agent guidance — AEGIS

AEGIS is an experimental privacy/anonymous-network research lab with protocol implementations, validation reports, and benchmark artifacts.

## Skill routing
- Anonymous routing, cryptography, metadata leakage, peer/RPC boundaries, or privacy claims: read .agents/skills/privacy-protocol-review/SKILL.md.
- Benchmarks, experiments, acceptance criteria, result reports, or recovery artifacts: read .agents/skills/reproducible-security-experiments/SKILL.md.

## Validation
Keep claims bounded to the threat model and evidence. Record exact revisions and test commands; do not overwrite historical reports or raw artifacts to make a result appear cleaner.
